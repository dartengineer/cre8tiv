Social media/cultural sunday write up.jpeg
CHALLENGE: CAC Youth needed a Gen Z flyer that doesn't look like boring church design.
PROCESS: I used Ankara pattern inside typography, 3D podium blocks, dancing cutouts to create energy, and Gen Z copy.
OUTCOME: Approved instantly, used for DCC-wide promotion.



Father_s Day.PNG
CHALLENGE: Make Father's Day feel emotional, not cliché.
PROCESS: Used double-exposure — an older father's face carrying the memory of him walking with his child. Bold text for honor, red script for love, gold light for legacy.
OUTCOME: A cinematic tribute that got high shares/saves because it felt like a movie poster, not a church flyer.


SPONTANEOUS WORSHIP (JAN).PNG
CHALLENGE: Make a worship night flyer feel like an actual worship moment, not just an announcement.
PROCESS: I kept the pastor in full color and high light to make him the emotional focus, while the congregation behind is faded to black & white — it puts the viewer IN the room. Added light rays and glow to sell the "spontaneous, atmosphere" feeling. Bold white + red for "SPONTANEOUS WORSHIP" creates urgency, with time zones clean at the bottom for the global audience.
OUTCOME: A flyer that doesn't just inform, it makes you feel the worship before the event starts

BIG DEAL SOCIAL MEDIA POST_.PNG
CHALLENGE: How to sell 3 different combos for ₦6k without confusing the customer, while making it feel like a steal ("Spend 6k, eat 14k").
PROCESS: I used color-blocking for easy scanning — red, yellow, black for each combo. Alternated text and food image left-right to create rhythm and keep the eye moving down. Added "6k only" badge and real food tray photos to trigger hunger instantly. Clear hierarchy: price hook first, then what you get.
OUTCOME: Menu designed to drive quick decisions at the counter. Customer sees value in 3 seconds, not 30.

APRIL 8TH LIVE IMAGE POST COPY.PNG
CHALLENGE: A real estate brand wanted to celebrate Mother's Day without losing the sales message. How do you honor moms AND still sell the estate?
PROCESS: I turned the number "2" into her journey. Inside the curve: mom cooking, helping kids with homework, doing laundry — "She's there all the way." Outside the curve: the finished estate, peaceful, with a happy mom walking. It connects her daily sacrifice to the reward of a good home.
OUTCOME: The design positioned Meridian not just as houses, but as a home that rewards a mother's effort. Emotional + commercial in one poster.


PRAISE PARTY.JPEG
CHALLENGE: Make a kids' Thanksgiving church flyer feel like a real party, not boring.
PROCESS: Used loud colors, chunky block type, speaker graphics + joyful B&W kids cutouts for contrast and energy.
OUTCOME: Fun, high-energy poster that stood out on socials and print.


THIS IS BRANDS AND LOGO SECTION
LELE BRAND WRITE UP
CHALLENGE: Build a luxury skincare identity that feels soft, natural and premium — not clinical or harsh. It needed to communicate "gentle, nature-inspired ritual" in a crowded skincare market.
PROCESS: Customized a serif wordmark with a leaf hidden inside the 'e' to signal nature. Paired it with airy spacing, a dark silk background for luxury feel, and soft, calm storytelling copy.
OUTCOME: A minimal, elegant brand that looks high-end, feels natural, and sells self-care as a ritual — "Soft skin. Pure ingredients. Natural glow."


BLOC BRAND WRITE UP
Challenge: Create a contemporary headwear brand that feels confident, street-inspired and stylish — not just another cap logo. It needed to stand out while staying minimal.
Process: Designed a bold, custom sans-serif wordmark for strength. Integrated a cap brim above the type with a blue under-brim accent as a subtle signature. Linked the O-C to show continuity and flow.
Outcome: A clean, modern, instantly recognizable identity that says "cap brand" without needing an extra icon — built for everyday style and streetwear culture.
